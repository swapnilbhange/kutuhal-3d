# Copy-paste prompts

Attach your script first, then paste one of these. Replace the `<…>`.

## Full video, defaults
> Make a Kutuhal 3D short from this script. 9:16, about <45> seconds, midnight style, captions on, generated music and SFX. Send me the MP4, the SRT and the narration.

## With creative direction
> Use Kutuhal 3D on the attached script. The main character is <Maya, a young woman with a bun and a coral top>. Recurring prop: <a wall clock>. Tone: <curious and gentle>. Keep every shot to one idea, title card on the line that names the concept, end card with the last line. 9:16, 1080p.

## Choose the voice first
> Start the Kutuhal 3D pipeline for this script but stop after generating voice samples. Render the first sentence in all available voices so I can listen and pick.

## Plan only (approve before building)
> Analyse this script with Kutuhal 3D and show me the plan only: bible, shot list with times, music moods. Don't render yet.

## Compare looks
> Using the project we just made, show me the five style presets on three key frames, then wait for my choice.

## Draft quickly, then go high quality
> Make a 540p draft first so I can review pacing. After I approve, render the final in 1080p.

## 16:9 version
> Re-use this project and make a 16:9 YouTube version. Re-check the framing of every shot.

## Audio only changes
> Same video, but calmer music (mood: calm), SFX 3 dB quieter, no ambience. Re-mix only, don't re-render.

## Own voice-over
> Here is my recorded narration and its SRT. Use Kutuhal 3D to build the video around it (don't use TTS). 9:16, midnight style.

## Own music
> Use the attached track as the music at –20 dB instead of generated music, ducked under the voice.

## Fix one shot
> In the finished project, shot <4> has the title overlapping the clock. Move the title up and shrink the clock, check it on a still, then re-render and re-mix.

## Batch series
> I will send several scripts. For each one use the same character, set and style as this project, and deliver one MP4 per script.

## Tips
- Say what you *don't* want too ("no music", "no zoom effects").
- If you are not around, say "decide for me and tell me what you chose" – Claude will state the assumptions at the top and continue.
- Claude can't hear audio: judge the voice and music yourself, then ask for a change.
