"How long will it take?" "Two hours, tops."

So you plan your whole evening around it. Then reality shows up, and two hours quietly becomes five.

This is the planning fallacy. We picture the best case, and forget how long things really took before.

The fix? Check your past. See how long similar tasks actually took, then add a buffer.

Plan with history, not hope.
